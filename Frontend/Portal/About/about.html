CREATE Hub Ghana
About CREATE Hub Ghana We're a hybrid organization bridging the gap between technology innovation and social impact in Ghana.
Our Mission
To empower Ghana's digital transformation by delivering innovative technology solutions to businesses while simultaneously nurturing the next generation of STEM leaders through comprehensive education and mentorship programs.
Our Vision
A Ghana where technology drives sustainable economic growth and every young person has the opportunity to become a digital innovator, creating solutions that benefit their communities and the world.
Our Hybrid Model Sustainable impact through commercial success and social responsibility
Commercial Branch
AI solutions and consulting services
Smart farming technology
Custom software development
Revenue funds our social programs
Non-Profit Branch
STEM education programs
Youth mentorship initiatives
Community technology centers
Scholarship and career support
Our Values The principles that guide everything we do
Innovation
We embrace cutting-edge technology to solve real-world problems and create meaningful impact.
Inclusion
We believe in equal access to technology education regardless of background or circumstances.
Excellence
We maintain the highest standards in everything we do, from our programs to our partnerships.
Transparency
We operate with complete openness about our impact, finances, and decision-making processes.
